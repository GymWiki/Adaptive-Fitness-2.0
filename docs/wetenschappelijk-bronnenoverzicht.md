## Trainingservaring & programma-ontwerp (novice/intermediate/advanced)

- **American College of Sports Medicine (2009). "Progression Models in Resistance Training for Healthy Adults." Position Stand, *Medicine & Science in Sports & Exercise*.** https://tourniquets.org/wp-content/uploads/PDFs/ACSM-Progression-models-in-resistance-training-for-healthy-adults-2009.pdf
  Kernaanbeveling (evidence-graded): **novice** → 2-3 dagen/week, full body; **intermediate** (~6+ maanden ervaring) → 4 dagen/week, upper/lower-split; **advanced** → 4-6 dagen/week, split-routine (1-3 spiergroepen per sessie).
- NSCA, "Determination of Resistance Training Frequency." https://www.nsca.com/education/articles/kinetic-select/determination-of-resistance-training-frequency/
